========================================================================
LIFE OS 1.0 — SIMPLE 1-CLICK NETLIFY DEPLOYMENT GUIDE
========================================================================

Follow these 7 simple steps to deploy your private Life OS web application for FREE:

1. Open your web browser and go to:
   https://app.netlify.com/drop

2. Log in or create a FREE Netlify account (takes 30 seconds).

3. Open File Explorer on your computer and locate this "deploy" folder:
   c:\Users\alkam\life tracker App\deploy

4. Drag the "deploy" folder and drop it into the box on the Netlify Drop webpage.

5. Netlify will build your site in ~5 seconds and give you a live link:
   (Example: https://your-life-os.netlify.app)

6. Open that live link on your iPhone or Android phone.

7. Tap your browser menu and choose "Add to Home Screen" to install it as a mobile app!

========================================================================
NOTE ON PRIVACY & LOCAL STORAGE:
All data logged in this application is saved locally in your phone's browser
(LocalStorage). Your personal data will NOT be visible to anyone else.
